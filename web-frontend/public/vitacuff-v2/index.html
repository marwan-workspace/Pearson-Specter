A medicine cabinet that rides on your wrist

VitaCuff is a wrist unit for astronauts. It straps over the veins on the inside of your wrist, watches your vitals around the clock and injects each dose on time.

Scroll to see how it works. Drag to turn the model.

It reads your body all day

Sensors under the straps sit against the wrist and report to the screen on top and to the flight surgeon on the ground.

Three syringes, issued with your access card

  1. Tap your access card at the ship's dispenser.
  2. It issues the prefilled syringes prescribed for you.
  3. Push up to three into the brass sockets at the hand end of the unit, needle first.
  4. At each set time the injector head, resting on the veins of your wrist, delivers the dose.
